How the license works.

One quick online step to activate, then everything runs offline — no account, no server to wait on.

1 Buy once

A one-time payment through our checkout. You get a receipt and your license key by email.

2 Paste your key

In MouseGestures, open Preferences → License, paste the key, and click Activate.

3 Unlocked, offline

Activation verifies your purchase once online, then MouseGestures works fully offline. Use the same key on every Mac you own.

Good to know.

Is it really a one-time purchase?

Yes. One payment, no subscription. Your license doesn't expire and works on future versions of MouseGestures 1.x.

I'm already in the trial — do I lose my settings?

No. Activating a license just flips the trial to Pro in place. Every gesture, profile, and setting stays exactly as you left it.

Do I need an account?

No account with us, ever. A purchased key verifies once online at activation (through our payment processor, Lemon Squeezy), then works fully offline after that — nothing else phones home.

Can I use it on more than one Mac?

Yes — the license is per-person, not per-machine. Activate it on any Mac you personally use.

What if my key doesn't work?

Email support@mousegestures.app with your order details and we'll sort it out.
